Help in menus and dropdowns appears beside the choices when there's room. In the Help menu, you can read what
Announcements does while the neighboring entries stay visible. Help for the host rail and sidebar rows also sits
to the right of its own column. Top controls and dialog buttons prefer the space below them. List help can sit
just outside launcher suggestions and lists in dialogs. The same rules apply to keyboard help.
