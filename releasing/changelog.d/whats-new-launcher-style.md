What's new uses the session launcher's look. The header shows your upgrade range and how many highlights are new,
changed and fixed, and each release shows its own total change count in place of the single total at the bottom.

Context for curation: entries now carry a colored category mark instead of the bullets an earlier release restored, a
highlight that opens with a bold sentence shows that sentence as its headline, and the screenshot link reads
"Screenshot" instead of "See screenshot". Every highlight was already shown in full before this change.
