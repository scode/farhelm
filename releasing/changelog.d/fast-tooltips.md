Hover text now appears after about a third of a second instead of the second or more the browser took, and sits just
above what you are pointing at in a small box that matches the app's theme. It also appears when you move to a control
with the keyboard, and shows at once as you move from one icon or button to the next. This covers every hover text the
app already had: the status dot, the agent and permission marks, the activity time, folders and commands, the restart
buttons, the text size buttons and the rest.
