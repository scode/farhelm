On a Mac, the desktop app no longer keeps the system busy and drains the battery while sessions are running. The green
dot beside a running session used to pulse smoothly, which made macOS redraw the whole Farhelm window at the display's
full refresh rate the entire time, even with the window hidden. The dot, and the other pulsing and spinning indicators,
now change in a few small steps per second, and all of them stand still whenever the Farhelm window is not the one you
are using. Reduce Motion still turns them off entirely.
