You can switch sessions and act on other sessions while stopping, restarting, replacing, deleting or launching a session;
Stop shows **Stopping…**, and results stay on the session's row and appear at the top when you return. Pending launches
hide the launcher and show a progress pill after about two seconds, with failures or questions bringing your choices
back.

A finished New or Clone leaves you where you are if you have carried on elsewhere, with a brief Started report;
Replace follows its source only while you are still on it, and completions preserve your focus, panels and menus.
