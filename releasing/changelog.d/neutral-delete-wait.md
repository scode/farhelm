While a deleted session waits for its agent to stop, the "Stopping agent…" notice over its terminal, the words that
replace its title in the session list, and the notice at the top of the open session are now blue instead of red. The
wait is what you just asked for, and the red made it look as if something had gone wrong. The session's row in the
list is no longer tinted while it waits; the spinner and words in place of its title mark it instead.
