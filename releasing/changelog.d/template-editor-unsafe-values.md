The template editor now shows hidden and direction-changing characters in a template's model, folder or repository, and in its launch and resume commands, as visible codes such as `<U+200B>` instead of hiding them. A template saved from a session cloned from another host could otherwise look different from what it launches. Such a field can't be edited in place; remove it with ✕ and add it again to change it.
