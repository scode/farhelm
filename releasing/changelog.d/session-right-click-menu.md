You can now right-click a session in the session list to open its actions menu at the pointer, the same menu its ⋯
button opens. Right-clicking a group header opens the group's menu, and Shift+F10 opens the menu from the keyboard.
With several sessions marked, right-clicking one of them opens a menu for all of them, with Restart/Replace, Stop and
Delete as well as the actions that change their pins, groups, mutes and read state.

Right-clicking a session that is not marked opens that session's own menu and leaves the marks alone. With only one
session marked, right-clicking it opens its own menu. The ⋯ button still shows on hover and focus.
