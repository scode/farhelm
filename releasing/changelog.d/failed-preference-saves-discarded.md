When the app cannot save one of your settings to the helm, such as the session list order, compact layout, the
quick-launch mark or a dismissed hint, the change no longer comes back later on its own. It stays in effect until the
app next reads your settings from the helm, and is then gone. Before, the app kept the unsaved setting and sent it again
after you signed in again.

Renaming a template whose quick-launch mark cannot be moved now shows the mark where the helm has it, instead of on the
new name.
