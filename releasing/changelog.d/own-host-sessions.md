You can now let agents manage sessions on their own host without asking: turn on **Manage sessions on this host
without asking** in the host's settings, or choose **Always allow on &lt;host&gt; only** on an approval card. It
starts off on every host. Requests for other hosts still ask; YOLO and command launches keep their
separate permission, and the helm must be reachable.
