A new **?** button at the top of the sidebar, to the right of the gear, opens a small menu with **send feedback** and
**documentation**. Send feedback lets you tell Farhelm's maintainer something privately, without an account: you type a
message and, if you like, how to reach you, and the dialog shows the only other things that go with it (the Farhelm
version, desktop app or web UI, and your operating system). Nothing is sent until you select send. If sending fails, your
text stays in the dialog.
