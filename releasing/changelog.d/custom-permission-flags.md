You can supply your own permission flags for Claude, Codex, Grok, Muse, Cursor and OMP; Farhelm does not check them and always treats these sessions as YOLO. The flags you type, including unfinished quotes and spacing, are kept when you pick a model through search or reselect the same agent. After upgrading, update remote hosts before using them; older Farhelm releases cannot read sessions or templates saved with custom flags (the missing downgrade path is accepted).
