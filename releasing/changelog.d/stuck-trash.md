A bell above Hosts now shows failed Trash cleanup and lets you retry or open Trash. Read notices stay listed quietly
until the files are gone, then can be cleared.

Trash keeps the failed checkout and its reason visible too. Files may already be partly removed once cleanup starts;
Farhelm never changes their permissions to force removal. Cleanup notices retain their history while a host is
unreachable and share read and resolved state between clients.
