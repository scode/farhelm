Settings and dialogs now share consistent headings and action styling in dark and light mode. Session actions and
template number shortcuts explain each choice, with destructive actions separated and marked in red.

Dialog buttons follow their visual order, with Cancel before the main action. Trash keeps its totals and global actions
together in the pinned header.

The session launcher groups its fields into labeled rows and keeps the target summary and action buttons in a visible
footer while the fields scroll. Enter in the folder field closes its suggestions when it submits a launch, including
when the launch is refused, so the controls below remain reachable.
