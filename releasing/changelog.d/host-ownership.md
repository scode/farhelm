A host now remembers which Farhelm manages it. Add host asks before taking over a host managed by another Farhelm.
You can also choose Take over in Hosts, including for this machine after losing your Farhelm registry.

Hosts without a recorded owner are claimed by the first Farhelm that connects, including after an upgrade.
Taking over ends the previous Farhelm's connection and leaves the host's sessions running.
