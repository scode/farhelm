On macOS, re-running the installer no longer deletes a `~/Applications/Farhelm.app` it did not build, such as one you built yourself or customised, just because its settings mention Farhelm; it now refuses and asks you to rename it. The installer also now rebuilds its own app when an interrupted uninstall had already removed part of it, which it used to refuse. The app the installer built before it started recording its own files (releases from early September 2026) is still recognised and replaced.
