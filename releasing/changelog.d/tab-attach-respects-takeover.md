A terminal tab no longer takes a session away from the window or device you are using. A tab that started attaching in
another window right after you took control in this one, or a tab you opened on another device while a window here was
reconnecting after sleep or a network drop, used to silently take the session back, detaching the window you were
actually using. That other window now shows the tab as "Detached" with take control, like the rest of its terminals.
