Muse sessions can now resume their conversation with Restart after the first prompt, and Muse can handle `$farhelm`
requests. This requires Muse 1.4.14 or later; existing Muse sessions must be recreated with Clone or Replace with to
gain Restart.

Farhelm also reads Muse's working indicator and idle prompt, and shows waiting for its directory-trust question.
Questions and approval dialogs during a conversation have not been verified.
