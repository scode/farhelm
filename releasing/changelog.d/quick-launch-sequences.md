You can now combine launch templates from the keyboard: Cmd+X in the Mac app, or Ctrl+Shift+X in a browser, followed by
their number keys and Enter. A pill shows the templates in order, and Escape cancels; single-key quick launches still
work as before. Partial templates can hold number keys too; pressed alone, they open the session launcher with a brief
hint explaining how to combine them.
