# A confirmed bulk target excluded as Deleting keeps its old mark

Reviewed commit: 00de0b6c9cf206f1a7c9c5ad1c76b5b10eaf85db

## TLDR

Source-confirmed review record: after confirming a bulk Stop or Delete, sessions refused because their environment has started deleting remain marked while the successful sibling targets clear. If every confirmed target is refused this way, the entire old marking remains. This makes the accepted batch cleanup depend on the refusal reason, despite no newer marking choice.
