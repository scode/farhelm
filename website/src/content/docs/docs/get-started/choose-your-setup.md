The first decision a new user has to make, and the one most likely to go wrong without help: where the helm runs. Two
setups, each with its own short walkthrough:

- Your Mac is your main machine: run the desktop app, which starts the helm and makes the Mac a host.
- The helm runs on a Linux machine: open its web UI from your laptop through an SSH port forward.

The page explains each choice from the user's side (what you run, where your sessions live, what happens when your
laptop sleeps) and links to [The pieces](/docs/how-it-works/the-pieces/) for anyone who wants the model behind it.
