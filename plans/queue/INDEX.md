# Plans queue

One line per plan that has not landed, in queue order, oldest first. `plans/AGENTS.md` describes the states; only `scripts/plans-queue.py` changes them, and planning PRs add new `[pending]` (or, when the maintainer asks, `[held]`) lines, at the end unless the maintainer places them elsewhere. A landed plan moves to `plans/REPORTS.md` until the maintainer has reviewed its report. This file is excluded from dprint so a line is never rewrapped.

- [held] `nonblocking-session-actions.md` — Pending Stop, Restart, Replace, Delete and launches no longer block switching sessions or acting on other ones; the acted-on session shows "Stopping…" and keeps its result after a switch
- [pending] `podman-environments.md` — A rootless Podman container on one of your hosts as a session's own machine, built from a shared recipe with Farhelm inside and the checkout cloned inside; sandbox and container sessions share one provider abstraction in code; today's containerized sessions become delete-only (after `sandbox-sessions.md`)
- [pending] `environment-agent-operations.md` — Agents in sandbox and container sessions can ask Farhelm to act within their own environment, environments live until their last session is deleted, and agents elsewhere can launch sessions in new environments behind an always-asking approval card (after `podman-environments.md`)
- [pending] `sandbox-stop-click-guards.md` — A sandbox session's Stop and suspend keeps the no-tabs and agent-ended guards the click saw, so a refresh during its activity check cannot close a new tab or stop a restarted agent unasked
- [complete] `user-tmux-jobs-outlive-cleanup.md` — Jobs that your own tmux moved out of a session may keep running after Stop, Restart, Delete or closing a tab on Linux hosts with systemd, as they would outside Farhelm; the pre-kill tmux check and its extra sweep go away
- [complete] `mac-system-stat.md` — Update, setup and uninstall on a remote Mac keep working when GNU coreutils come first on the SSH PATH, because Farhelm always calls the system `/usr/bin/stat` there
- [blocked] `tensorlake-skip-ssh-agent.md` — Tensorlake builds and host connections offer Farhelm's own key first and no longer consult your ssh-agent, so a personal key loaded there cannot get the login refused
- [complete] `hosts-suspend-confirm-slot.md` — The Hosts panel's Suspend question acts only on the answer to the question you saw, like host Remove
